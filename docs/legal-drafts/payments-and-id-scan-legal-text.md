# Legal text for payments (Stripe) and the ID card scan

Status: applied to the unpublished `datenschutz` and `agb` pages in the back office (dev and production) on 4 November 2026. Not legal advice. German is the main language; the German was written by Claude from the English and needs a German speaker. Things in [square brackets] are for the owner or the lawyer.

What changed besides the sections below: the privacy table has four new rows (date of birth; requests, quotes, bookings, chat and reviews; payment data; the ID card photo), Stripe is listed as a processor in privacy section 3 and the ID scan in the OpenRouter line, and privacy section 6 (retention) has two new lines (date of birth; payment records, invoices and receipts, period to be confirmed). The privacy page did not describe bookings, payments or invoices before.

## Terms, new section 5b

### English

**5b. Filling in your details from your ID card (optional)**
In the app, under Account, you can photograph your ID card to fill in your name, date of birth and address instead of typing them. This is your choice: you can always type the details in yourself. The photo is sent once, with settings for no training and no retention, to the AI model service described in section 5a, which suggests the values. We do not store the photo, and we do not read or store the card number or other numbers. You see every value, you can change it, and nothing is saved until you tap Save. The date of birth is private, is never shown to other users, and is used to check that providers meet the age requirement. You can change or remove it in the app, or delete your account. More in the privacy policy, section 4c.

## Privacy policy, new sections 4b and 4c

### English

**4b. Payments**
Payments for bookings are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. [confirm legal entity and address], an independent payment provider. Customers enter their card details on Stripe's own payment page; we never see or store card numbers. A provider who wants to receive payouts gives Stripe the details Stripe needs to verify identity and bank account, in a form hosted by Stripe; Stripe is responsible for those checks and for that data under its own privacy policy [add the link]. We receive from Stripe the status of each payment and payout and keep the amount, the time, the status, the booking it belongs to, the commission and any refund, and we create invoices and receipts from them. Stripe may process data outside the EU on the basis of an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses [confirm]. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR. We keep payment records, invoices and receipts for the period the law requires for accounting and tax records [period to be confirmed by the tax adviser].

**4c. Filling in your details from your ID card (optional)**
In the app, under Account, you can photograph your ID card to fill in your name, date of birth and address instead of typing them. Only you start this, and only if you have accepted the Terms. The photo is sent once, with the settings for no training and no retention, to the same AI model service as in section 4a, which suggests the values. We do not store the photo, and the card number and other numbers are not read or stored. You see every value, you can change it, and nothing is saved until you tap Save. What you save is stored in your account like anything you type in yourself. The date of birth is private, never shown to other users, and used to check that providers meet the age requirement. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by using the scan, and Art. 6(1)(b). You can always type the details in yourself instead, and you can change or delete your date of birth in the app or delete your account. [Confirm with the lawyer and in the data protection impact assessment.]

### Deutsch

**4b. Zahlungen**
Zahlungen für Buchungen werden von Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. [Rechtsträger und Adresse bestätigen] abgewickelt, einem eigenständigen Zahlungsdienstleister. Kunden geben ihre Kartendaten auf der eigenen Zahlungsseite von Stripe ein; wir sehen oder speichern keine Kartennummern. Wir erhalten von Stripe den Status jeder Zahlung und Auszahlung und speichern Betrag, Zeitpunkt, Status, die zugehörige Buchung, die Provision und etwaige Erstattungen; daraus erstellen wir Rechnungen und Belege. Stripe kann Daten außerhalb der EU verarbeiten, auf Grundlage eines Angemessenheitsbeschlusses oder von Standardvertragsklauseln [bestätigen]. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b und c DSGVO. Zahlungsunterlagen, Rechnungen und Belege bewahren wir so lange auf, wie das Gesetz es für Buchführung und Steuer verlangt [Dauer vom Steuerberater bestätigen lassen].

**4c. Angaben vom Ausweis ausfüllen (optional)**
In der App kannst du unter Konto deinen Ausweis fotografieren, damit Name, Geburtsdatum und Adresse ausgefüllt werden, statt sie einzutippen. Das startest nur du, und nur wenn du die AGB akzeptiert hast. Rechtsgrundlage: deine Einwilligung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO), erteilt durch die Nutzung des Scans, und lit. b. Du kannst deine Angaben jederzeit selbst eintippen, dein Geburtsdatum in der App ändern oder löschen oder dein Konto löschen. [Mit dem Anwalt und in der Datenschutz-Folgenabschätzung bestätigen.]
