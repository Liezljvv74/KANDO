## 2. Ranking: how search results are ordered

SkillWeb shows providers who match your search. This page explains in plain words how the results are ordered (Regulation (EU) 2019/1150, Article 5).

**What decides the order**
1. **Distance.** If you enter a postcode, the providers closest to it come first. A provider who works on site appears only if your postcode is inside the area they have set (their service radius). Providers who work remotely always appear, after the nearby ones.
2. **Name.** Providers at the same distance, and all results when no postcode is entered, are sorted alphabetically by their public name.

**What does not change the order**
- Payment. Nobody can pay to appear higher, and SkillWeb has no paid placement or advertising.
- Ratings and reviews. They do not exist yet; when we add them, this page will be updated before they affect the order.
- The category or the number of services a provider offers (these only decide whether the provider matches your filter).

**Who appears at all**
Only published profiles appear. A profile is published when it is complete and the documents required for its services have been checked by SkillWeb staff. A profile that is suspended or switched off does not appear.

**Filters you choose**
Category, text search and postcode only narrow the list; they do not reorder it, except that the postcode sets the distance.

We will update this page whenever the way results are ordered changes. Last updated: [date of publication].
